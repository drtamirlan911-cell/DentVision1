# AI Employee Product Roadmap

The execution layer is now the primary product path. Work should be delivered in vertical slices rather than broad audits.

## Slice 1 — Identity
- Role-specific AI employee identity in AI Workspace.
- Mission and autonomy are visible.

## Slice 2 — Work
- Durable task lifecycle.
- Task feed in the workspace.
- Priority and approval state visible.

## Slice 3 — Act
- Approval action calls the existing permission/execution boundary.
- Routine actions can execute automatically according to the role contract.
- Clinical/high-risk actions remain human-controlled.

## Slice 4 — Verify
- Tool result is verified.
- Failed actions remain actionable.
- Completed actions create an auditable outcome.

## Slice 5 — Vertical CRM workflows
1. Patient intake → Doctor AI Employee.
2. Appointment reminder/no-show → Reception AI Employee.
3. Lab deadline/status → Lab AI Employee.
4. Payment overdue → Finance AI Employee.

## Slice 6 — Premium workspace
- Unified inbox/tasks/approvals/timeline.
- Evidence and reason shown beside recommendations.
- Responsive desktop/mobile parity.

## Release rule
No feature is considered complete because it renders. It must have a useful state transition, a safe action boundary, and a verifiable outcome.
